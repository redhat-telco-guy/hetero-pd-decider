Heat test

    Puts steady work straight onto the cards (not through the router) so you can watch temperatures and fans react below. For Gaudi, the model server is started first (several minutes of loading and warm-up) and scaled back to 0 when the test ends. Safety: the test stops itself at 78 °C on a Gaudi and a few degrees below an A30's slowdown point; the watchdog still stops everything at 80 °C.
